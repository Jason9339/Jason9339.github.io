Zih-Ci Lin

Research Assistant, National Chengchi University (NCCU), Taipei, Taiwan

Hi! I’m Zih-Ci Lin, but you can call me Jason. I’m a full-time research assistant at National Chengchi University (NCCU) in Taipei, Taiwan, where I received my B.S. degrees in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Applications in 2026. I’m interested in computer vision and multimodal learning, especially how vision and language models can work together to understand documents and images in the real world. I’m applying to M.S. programs in Computer Science for Fall 2027.

Research interests

  • Computer vision
  • Multimodal large language models
  • Document understanding and OCR

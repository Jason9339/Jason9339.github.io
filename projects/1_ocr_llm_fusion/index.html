OCR–LLM Fusion for Traditional Chinese Historical Documents

2024 – 2026 · Fusing an OCR model with a frozen language model at decoding time to reduce character errors on Traditional Chinese historical manuscripts (ICPR 2026).

Undergraduate research · Visual Information Processing Lab, NCCU · 2024 – 2026
Advisor: Prof. Wen-Hung Liao · NSTC-funded project
Publication: Zih-Ci Lin and Wen-Hung Liao. “Decoding-Time Fusion of OCR and Large Language Models for Traditional Chinese Historical Document Recognition.” In Proceedings of the 28th International Conference on Pattern Recognition (ICPR 2026), LNCS vol. 16819, pp. 48–62. Springer, 2026. [DOI]

Problem

OCR for Traditional Chinese historical documents must recognize characters that are often degraded, rare, or visually ambiguous. This project investigates how linguistic context can help historical Chinese OCR: when a language model can correct the visual recognizer, and when it makes things worse.

Approach

Extending Generative Fusion Decoding (GFD) to Traditional Chinese historical OCR, the system fuses TrOCR hypotheses with a frozen language model in a UTF-8 byte-aligned beam search, so linguistic context is applied at decoding time without retraining the language model. To train and evaluate the system, I built a configurable synthetic-text generator that produces 4.1M historical-style image–text pairs, and assembled a manually annotated benchmark of 921 real-manuscript samples.

Results

Decoding-time fusion reduced the character error rate (CER) by 10.00% (relative) on real manuscripts and by up to 15.37% on semantically structured text. Ablation studies revealed failures caused by excessive language-model influence.

My role

First author. Extended Generative Fusion Decoding (GFD) to Traditional Chinese historical OCR, built the synthetic data generator and the manually annotated benchmark, and conducted all experiments.

**Role:** Independent Project<br>
**Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Jason9339/Wavelet-Noise-in-ray-tracing)

I implemented wavelet noise and Perlin noise as 3D textures in a ray tracer built on the _Ray Tracing in One Weekend_ series, and rendered both for side-by-side comparison.
I also analyzed the frequency spectra of 2D and 3D-sliced noise, reproducing the frequency-domain comparisons in the original wavelet noise paper to check its band-limited property.

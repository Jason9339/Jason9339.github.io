**Course:** Natural Language Processing · 2025<br>
**Role:** Team Leader (team of four)

### Problem

Can prompting strategies such as chain-of-thought make large language models reliable at Chinese logical reasoning?

### Approach

We curated 80 Chinese logic puzzles graded by difficulty and compared six prompting strategies, including chain-of-thought.

### Results

Prompting gains were limited and unstable on hard problems, while reasoning-oriented models stayed accurate.
This suggests that structural changes beyond prompt engineering are needed.
